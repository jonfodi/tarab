change through sound.

Improving our world starts with improving our inner world.

Our thoughts have real effects on the world outside us. In a good mood, we smile. We give others grace. When I'm happy on my bike, I thank people for not opening their doors on me, for letting me pass, for driving kindly. It makes me feel good. It makes them feel good, and makes them want to do it again.

This is how love defies physics. Love is not conserved. One small act in leads to so much positivity out.

Music is my key to it. I wake up with music already playing in my head. As a kid playing 3-on-3 hockey, a friend asked me why I was singing. I didn't even know I was.

Music lets us feel that love. It keeps us company when we're sad. It gives us the joy of finding something new, and a whole day that feels brighter for it.

One song, one dancefloor, one group of people, carrying it out into their lives. That's why I play music for people. That's why I made this.
