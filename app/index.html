        Set up tarab

        tarab downloads from Soulseek, a peer-to-peer network. Log in with your Soulseek account. New to Soulseek? Pick any unused username and a password: the first login creates the account.

        If you also use SoulseekQt or Nicotine+ with the same account, close them while tarab runs (an account can only be logged in once).

        Soulseek runs on sharing: everything comes from other users' computers. Many users let sharers skip their queues and refuse people who share nothing, so sharing makes your downloads faster. Other users can download the files in your music folder (never change or delete them). Only tracks that passed tarab's checks are in that folder. You can turn this off any time.
